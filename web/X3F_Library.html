The X3 way — every movement

Variable resistance loads you heavy in the strong range, light in the weak range. Keep constant tension — never lock out at the top, never rest at the bottom. Slow & controlled, ~2–3s each way. Do 15–40 full reps, then keep going with diminishing range — shorter and shorter partials — to true failure. The goal is exhausting the muscle, not hitting a number. (Bloom's burnout phase is built on exactly this.)
