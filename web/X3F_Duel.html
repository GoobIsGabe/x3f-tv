X3F DUEL

Tug-of-war against the machine. Every rep hauls the rope your way - and how far depends on how deep and how slow it was, not how many you can rattle off. Two to three seconds each way is the winning pace; anything quicker barely moves the rope. While you are loaded up the CPU can hardly pull at all, so the hardest part of your set is also your best defence.
