X3F RHYTHM

Two seconds up, two seconds down — on the beat. Notes fall to the metronome: take the top note by peaking your rep as it lands, and the bottom note by turning around without letting the band go slack.

Pull as hard as you can. There is no ceiling here — a harder rep is always worth more, and once full range is gone every partial is worth more again.
