X3F FLOW

Controlled reps, the X3 way. The ribbon is your pacer - stay on or above it, two to three seconds each way. Above is never wrong: beat the ribbon's peak and the ribbon climbs to meet you. Keep going until the range is gone, then keep going in the range you have left. Put the bar down and the set ends itself.
