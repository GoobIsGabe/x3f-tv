X3F BLOOM

Ride the glowing vine. Pull up into every bloom, ease down into every bud - never all the way to rest, keep the tension on. Two seconds up, two seconds down; speed is worth nothing here.

Every rep is measured and graded: full range, then mid range partials as the blooms drop, then weak range - as far as you can still move it. The vine follows you down. When the bar will not move at all, that is the set.
